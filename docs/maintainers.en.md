## Maintainers and contributors wanted

We welcome people who can help maintain Vyline on a continuing or temporary basis. Areas include Windows, Linux, and macOS maintenance; LINE SQUARE (OpenChat) research; debugging; issue triage; PR review; distribution; and translation.

These are unpaid volunteer opportunities. We are looking for help to improve the user experience. Short-term and task-specific contributions are welcome.

See the [Contribution Guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md) and propose your help through an issue or pull request. Do not post sessions, tokens, encryption keys, or user data in public issues.

## Optional support
